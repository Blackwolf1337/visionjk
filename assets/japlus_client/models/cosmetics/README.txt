Hats and capes for VisionJK (cgame/look/cg_look.c, docs/japlus/client.md "Hats and capes").

The models and pictures in hats/ and capes/ are from jaPRO (https://github.com/videoP/jaPRO) and TaystJK
(https://github.com/taysta/TaystJK, assets/japro/models/cosmetics), taken unchanged on 2026-10-17. The only
change: hats/helmet.JPG is named helmet.jpg. Their shaders that need more than the picture are in
shaders/vision_cosmetics.shader. Permission to ship them with VisionJK was given to the VisionJK project
(2026-10-17), for all of them, also for the pieces that come from other works.

Hats: afro, beard, bucket, cap, cringe, crown, fedora, fedora2, fedora3, fedora4, glasses, gradcap, headcrab,
horns, mario, mask, metalhelm, plaguemask, predatorhelm, pumpkin, santahat, sombrero, supersaiyan, tophat.
Capes: ak47, crowbar, goose, grogucape, royalcape, rpg, vadercape, yodacape.

mask and pumpkin came to jaPRO from Movie Battles II.

More can come in any pak: models/cosmetics/hats/<name>.md3 or capes/<name>.md3, and optionally
settings/cosmetics/hats/<name>.cosmetic with offsets per player model and skin (TaystJK's format).
