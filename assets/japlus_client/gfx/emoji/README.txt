Emoji of the chat box (cgame/chat/cg_chat.c, docs/japlus/client.md).

The pictures with plain names (smile.png ...) are from Noto Emoji by Google
(https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji, folder 2D/png/72), scaled to 64 x 64 (the image loader of the
game takes sides of a power of two only) and renamed to the names the chat box uses (":smile:" is smile.png).
The project says its image resources are under the Apache License 2.0; its LICENSE file, copied here as
LICENSE-noto-emoji.txt, is the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

The pictures with TaystJK's names (`kappa`.png, #`!B.png ...) are from TaystJK
(https://github.com/taysta/TaystJK, assets/japro/gfx/emoji), unchanged, 2026-10-18: those of its set whose code
the pictures of Noto do not have already, its emotes of Twitch among them. Permission to ship them with VisionJK
was given to the VisionJK project. In their names a ` is a colon of the code, a ~ is a >, and ! makes the next
letter a capital.
