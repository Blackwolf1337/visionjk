* [*] The snowball toy of issue #9 reworked (2026-11-02). The ball is a real model: the new tool `tools/toys/snowball.py` (no outside assets) makes `models/vision/toys/snowball/ball.md3` (an icosphere of 1280 triangles pushed in and out by value noise, the radius of the old ball, 3 units) and three rough lumps `chunk1-3.md3`, plus `snow.png` and its normal map `snow_n.png` (clumps from periodic noise, blue in the hollows). The ball tumbles along its flight path. The old sprite picture `gfx/vision/toys/snowball.png` and its maker in `tools/toys/textures.py` are removed. Two new shader keywords for model stages, lit per pixel by rd-vulkan-vision (parsed in the shared `rd-vanilla/tr_shader.cpp`, fields `wrapLight` and `sparkle` in `shaderStage_t`, `vkDraw_t::material2`; rd-vanilla ignores them): `wrapLight <amount>` lets the light wrap into the shadow side with a cool sky tint, as through snow (GPU Gems 1 chapter 16: N.L plus wrap over 1 plus wrap; the tint as in the snow of Batman: Arkham Origins), and `sparkle <strength>` adds glints of crystals (a point with a random facet in each cell of a grid in model space, after Shopf 2012 and Wang and Bowles 2016, faded where a cell is smaller than a pixel). Material `models/vision/toys/snowball/snow` in `shaders/vision_toys.shader`. The throw: while +v_snowball is held the own player raises the arm in the pose of a held thermal detonator (`TORSO_WEAPONREADY10`, `CG_SnowballTorsoAnim`), the ball sits on the right hand bone (`CG_SnowballHand`) and the weapon is put away meanwhile (`CG_SnowballHolding`); in first person the ball lies in the view and is pulled back as the throw is charged (`CG_SnowballViewBall`). Everybody who hears of a throw sees the throw animation `BOTH_THERMAL_THROW`. The burst: the powder effect stays (`effects/vision/snowball_hit.efx`, its old flat chunks and its sound block removed); 5 to 8 lumps of snow (the chunk models) fly off, bounce with little of their speed left, come to rest on a floor and melt away over 5 to 8 s (`Toys_Chunks`, `Toys_AddChunks`, a pool of 48). The splat: with rd-vulkan-vision a new kind of wet place `WET_SNOW` (`rd-common/tr_types.h`, `WET_MAX_SPOTS` now 128) is drawn by the wet pass (`shaders/wet.frag`): a thick layer of snow with clumps as bumps (the normal from the slope of the clump noise), lit as the snow of the ball (wrap light, blue in the hollows and where it is thin, glinting crystals); on a wall it slides down a little over 2.5 s and leaves a thinner smear above, it melts from its thin edges in over 25 s, and the ground under it is made wet by the water gun's code (`CG_WaterSoak`, `CG_WaterWetDrawn` in `cg_water.c`), which then dries as usual. Up to 16 splats (`Toys_AddSnows`). The hit point is put onto the surface (the ball is traced as a box, its centre was 3 units off the wall). Renderers without the wet pass keep the old splat mark. Snow on the view when the own face is hit: a new screen effect source `SCREENFX_SNOW` (`rd-common/tr_types.h`, `shaders/screenfx.frag`), announced by the engine cvar `v_screenFxSnow`: one to three large clumps and some small bits stick on the side the ball came from, with a ragged edge, clumpy relief lit from above as bright as the picture around is, and a thin slushy rim that bends the picture; they slide down slowly, melt from the edges in and turn into the drops of water of the water gun (`SCREENFX_DROP`), which run down and dry (`CG_ScreenFxSnow`, `CG_ScreenFxSnows`, `ScreenFx_AddDrop` in `cgame/screenfx/cg_screenfx.c`). Without it the old picture of snow on the screen is drawn. Sounds, synthesized in `tools/toys/sounds.py` (no recorded audio; the footstep sounds of the game are no longer used): `snow_pack.wav` (packing it when the charge starts), `snow_throw.wav`, `snow_hard1-3.wav` (on stone or metal), `snow_soft1-2.wav` (on soft ground, chosen by the material of the surface), `snow_body.wav` (on a player) and `snow_face.wav` (in the own face). The crunch is grains of noise in the two bands of snow of footstep synthesis (compression around 1.3 kHz and the crunch of crystals around 3 kHz, after Turchet et al.), with a low thump and the hiss of powder. Fix: while a toy (the water gun or a snowball) was in the hand, the weapon model was taken off every frame, the cgame forgot the weapon, and putting it back counted as a switch to the saber, which ignited with its sound (every frame with a snowball). The weapon a toy put away is now kept (`cg_toyHidWeapon` in `cg_players.c`), and the own weapon is not put back while a snowball is held (`cg_weapons.c`). Tested headless with 8x MSAA: hold, throw, flight, bursts and splats on a wall with lumps, sliding and melting, snow on the view melting into drops, the sounds loading and playing, no ignite sounds while a toy is held; checked in the game on a PC. Not tested: a hit on the floor, how another player sees throws and hits. The Linux and Windows builds compile.

* [*] The RC car toy of issue #9 reworked to look and feel like a modern game (2026-11-01). Model: a toy monster truck from Kenney's Toy Car Kit 1.0 (CC0), made by the new tool `tools/toys/rccar.py` into `models/vision/toys/rccar/body.md3` (paint, trim, glass; tags `tag_wheel_fl`, `tag_wheel_fr`, `tag_wheel_bl`, `tag_wheel_br`, `tag_head`, `tag_tail`) and `wheel.md3` (tire, rim). The tool sorts the faces into materials by the palette cell of the kit, gives each material its own texture coordinates and generates the textures with normal maps: candy paint with flakes and orange peel, blue grey plastic, knobby tire tread with side walls, brushed gold rims, dark glass, a lamp glow sprite, a smoke puff, a motion blurred tire and rim (`wheel_blur.skin`), and the marks of the tires (`track` tread imprint and `skid` rubber, as darkness maps). `read_obj` of `tools/toys/watergun.py` can now report the OBJ groups and `write_md3` of `tools/boombox/build.py` can write tags. Renderer: new shader stage keywords for models, `normalMap <image>` (never compressed) and `specular <strength> <exponent> <coat>` (shared `rd-vanilla/tr_shader.cpp`, new fields in `shaderStage_t`; rd-vanilla draws such stages as before). rd-vulkan-vision lights such lit stages per pixel (`VKD_MATERIAL`, `vkDraw_t::material`, `MaterialLight` in `shaders/generic.frag`): the light of the entity on the normal from the normal map, a tangent frame from screen space derivatives (Schueler's cotangent frame, so models need no tangents), a broad Blinn-Phong shine, a sharp clear coat over it and a Fresnel rim. The materials are in `shaders/vision_toys.shader`. Drawing: the body plus four wheel entities from the tags, the wheels rolled by the distance and the front ones steered (other players' cars steer by their heard turn, Ackermann); each wheel is built in the axes of the body and multiplied with them, so the wheels follow pitch and roll. Head lamps are glow sprites with a dynamic light on the floor ahead, tail lamps brighten while braking (also on other players' cars, from the heard speed), and the car has a blob shadow. Handling (`codemp/cgame/toys/cg_rccar.c`): a raycast vehicle at 120 steps a second, each wheel a ray down from its mount along the body with a spring (the car rests 1.65 units down, about 3.5 bounces a second), a bump stop and past it a solid stop that lifts the body out instead of letting the wheels sink into the floor. The dampers take speed away implicitly, from the real speed of each mount, so a landing does not kick the car back up. The body pitches and rolls by the wheel forces and leans by weight transfer on throttle, brakes and in turns. Tires take the sideways speed away at most as hard as the load allows (mu), past that the car slides; the turn is capped at what the grip holds, so it pushes wide at speed instead of tipping. Shift is a handbrake that drops the rear grip for drifts, Space hops, and in the air the throttle tips the nose up and the brakes down. The body is a box over the wheels (so slopes up to about 37 degrees and low steps meet the wheels first), with a step up for higher ones. The camera follows the turn of the car a little late. The help line is updated in English, German, French and Spanish (`tools/menus/texts.py`, `texts_more.py`, the regenerated `strings/*/vision.str`). Network: the `TOY_RC` message is unchanged (still the point on the ground under the car); other players' cars lean by the ground under their four wheels and by their turn, and a jump to a newly heard position fades out instead of snapping. Effects: tire marks in a pool of their own (512 pieces, not the shared 256 marks), strips from where each wheel was to where it is, cut to the level once with `R_MarkFragments` and faded over 20 s: the tread pressed into sand, dirt, mud, snow and gravel, and rubber from the rear tires on hard ground while sliding or braking hard. Dust of the ground comes from the material effects of the game, and tire smoke (`effects/vision/tire_smoke.efx`) rises while sliding on hard ground. Through the puddles of the water gun the wheels spray and send rings (`CG_WaterWheel` in `cg_water.c`), and the rear tires then leave wet tread tracks that dry (`CG_WaterTrack`, new wet place kind `WET_TRACK` in `rd-common/tr_types.h`, drawn in `shaders/wet.frag`). Fast wheels switch to the blurred skin. Sounds are synthesized in `tools/toys/sounds.py` (no recorded audio): `rc_motor1.wav` to `rc_motor4.wav` (electric motor whine with gear buzz at four speeds), `rc_scrub.wav`, `rc_crunch.wav`, `rc_servo.wav`, `rc_land1.wav`, `rc_land2.wav` and `rc_on.wav`. The motor crossfades the two loops its speed lies between and is louder under throttle; scrub plays while sliding on hard ground, crunch on dirt, the servo buzz when the front wheels swing, a thump on landing, and the receiver chirp replaces the mouse droid sounds. New engine function for the cgame: `ext.S_AddLoopingSoundVolume` (`S_AddLoopingSoundVolume` in `client/sound/snd_dma.cpp`, loops at a volume of their own for the mixer and v_spatial), announced by the cvar `v_cgameLoopVolume`; without it the car plays only its main motor loop. Tested headless only (gamescope, 8x MSAA): the look, driving, slides and skid marks, a ramp climb, drops and landings, puddle spray and wet tracks, and the game audio recorded and analysed; driven in the game on a PC. * [\*] Key login (`v_amkey`) is bound to the server by a host key (protocol version 2). The server makes a 32 byte random host key once and keeps it as base64 in `auth_host.key` in its game folder, next to `admin_keys.txt` (only when `admin_keys.txt` has keys; if the file can not be written the key is new at every start). It sends it with the nonce: `vs_auth nonce <nonce> <host key>`. The signed message has the host key in it, so a signature made for one server is no use on another. `vs_caps` is 399 now (was 143): bit 8 (256, `AUTH_CAPS_HOST`) is the key login with the host key. The client keeps the host key of every server address in `admin_hosts.txt` in its home folder (one line `<address> <host key>`, at most 8192 bytes, the oldest lines go first). The first login on an address keeps the key, like ssh `known_hosts`. A server that sends another key than the one kept for its address, or a key kept for another address, is not signed for and the player gets a message, also after the automatic login. `v_amkey trust` logs in and keeps the key the server sends now (for a server that was set up again, or one with a second address), and `v_amkey forget` forgets the host key of the address too. A server without a host key (older game module) is signed for only when the player typed `v_amkey login`, never by itself. Compatibility: a server of this version takes a version 1 signature (older clients) only when `vs_authHosts` is set. Without it an older client gets `vs_auth fail version` and is not logged in, so those players have to update, or the owner of the server sets `vs_authHosts`. Still open: the first manual login on a hostile server that the client never logged in to, and that has no `vs_authHosts`. Unit tests in `tests/vjk/auth.cpp`, and two local dedicated servers with a headless client (first login, next login, other key at a kept address, kept key at a new address, `v_amkey trust`, a server and a client of 0.2.8). See docs/japlus/security.md.
* [\*] Voice relay: from the server machine (the path of the web host, `vs_clientWebPort`) a datagram is taken with the token alone. More than 20 wrong tokens in a second from that machine now make the relay take nothing from it for 10 seconds, with one line in the security log. That stops the voice of browser clients for that time too. 2^32 tokens then take decades.
* [\*] The dedicated server catches SIGTERM and SIGINT (Linux, macOS) and quits at the start of the next frame as on `quit`: players are told, the game module ends and the web panel saves its sessions. A second signal while it quits ends the process at once. The warning "select() syscall failed: Interrupted system call" is no longer printed for a signal.

* [\*] rd-vulkan-vision, Vulkan and OpenGL ES device: sprites, lines, saber glow, oriented quads, cylinders, the bolts of lightning, the sparks of effects and the rain and snow are made in a vertex shader (`sprite.vert`). The CPU still works out where each one is, but writes one record a quad instead of four vertices and six indexes. `v_vkSprites 0` (cheat) builds the corners on the CPU as before. On the fight demo `clip_brawl1` the triangles built on the CPU in a frame went from 5505 to 1264, the pictures differ in 0.04 % of the pixels (0.75 % with sparks, which are random, as two runs of the old way are).
* [+] rd-vulkan-vision: the grass (surface sprites of the level) is placed once when the level loads and drawn from buffers with `grass.vert`, with the same fade, wind and sway. The ground under it is drawn from the buffer of the level too. `deformVertexes autosprite` and `autosprite2` on the level, saber marks that grow or fade, and vertex lit surfaces with several light styles also left the CPU. `v_vkGrass 0` (cheat, latched) places the grass on the CPU every frame.
